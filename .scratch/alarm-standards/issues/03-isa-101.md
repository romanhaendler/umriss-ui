## Comments

**Delivered (2026-09-24).** `docs/design-language.md` has a section "Colour
for the abnormal (ISA-101)": grey is the normal state, colour is for the
abnormal, never colour alone - and where umriss departs (the danger button and
destructive menu item colour a consequence; the "ok" verdict word is tinted
green).

**A fully automatic check is not feasible, and the check says so.** Whether a
colour stands beside a word is a property of the rendered page, and that
depends on the caller's content: a `Badge` of tone "danger" with no children
is red without a word, and no stylesheet can prevent it. So the check is the
narrowest one that holds: `packages/core/tests-unit/verdictColour.test.ts`
registers every stylesheet of the library (core, table, schedule,
calculation, charts - 21 today) that reaches for a danger, warning or success
token, each with what carries its meaning without colour. A new stylesheet
with a verdict colour fails until it is written down; a stale entry fails;
the detector is tested to fire. Its ceiling - per stylesheet, not per rule -
stands in the test. Writing the register found one honest gap, named in its
entry: the meter (`DataViz`) fills in a tone and its optional figure is the
percentage, not the verdict, so the word for its tone is the caller's.
