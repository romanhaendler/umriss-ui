# @umriss-ui/calculation API

Generated by the props step (`pnpm --filter @umriss-ui/calculation props`); do not edit. First sentences only; the full text is `docs/llms-full.md`.

## Components

- Calculation — A derivation the package works out itself and shows as a statement, from the givens up to the result: one quantity as its child, written with `Given`, the operators, `Chain` and `Ref`.
- Chain — A calculation read top to bottom, as on paper.
- Difference — The first operand minus every other: a − b − c.
- DividedBy — Divides the named value before it by its operand.
- Given — A number the calculation does not derive.
- Interim — Names the value of a chain where it stands.
- Minus — Takes its operand from the value before it.
- Plus — Adds its operand to the value before it.
- Product — The product of two or more operands.
- Quotient — Exactly two operands: the first divided by the second.
- Ref — A quantity defined elsewhere in the calculation, standing as an operand.
- Sum — The sum of its operands, however many: rows from data, one or none included.
- Times — Multiplies the named value before it by its operand.

## Types

- CalculationProps — `className` and `style` size and place the surface; every other attribute - `aria-label` first of all - lands on the list the surface holds.
- ChainOperandProps — The props of `Plus`, `Minus`, `Times` and `DividedBy`: a given written in place - `label` and `value` - or a quantity as the child.
- ChainProps — The props of `Chain`.
- GivenProps — The props of `Given`: a quantity's, with the number it starts from and where and when that number came from.
- Metric — One of several numbers every quantity carries side by side (ADR-0038): headcount and full-time equivalents of the same teams. [id, label, unit, decimals]
- MetricValues — A given's numbers where the calculation has metrics: one per metric id, `null` where the number is absent.
- OperatorProps — The props of `Sum`, `Difference`, `Product` and `Quotient`: a quantity's, with its operands as children.
- QuantityProps — What every quantity - given or derived - can say about itself.
- RefProps — The props of `Ref`.

## Props

### Calculation

#### props (CalculationProps)

- children: ReactNode — The result: exactly one quantity - a tree (`<Sum>`, `<Difference>`, `<Product>`, `<Quotient>` with their operands, `<Given>` as leaves) or a `<Chain>`, the two mixing freely; `<Ref>` for a quantity defined elsewhere.
- metrics?: readonly Metric[] — Several numbers per quantity, side by side - headcount and full-time equivalents of the same teams ([ADR-0038](https://github.com/romanhaendler/umriss-ui/blob/main/docs/adr/0038-a-calculation-can-carry-several-metrics.md)).
- density?: "regular" | "compact" — `"compact"` sets the lines as close as a compact table's rows: smaller type and less room above and below, under a finger still tall enough to tap. (default: the density of the `UmrissProvider`, else `"regular"`)
