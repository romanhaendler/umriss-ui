# @umriss-ui/core API

Generated by the props step (`pnpm --filter @umriss-ui/core props`); do not edit. First sentences only; the full text is `docs/llms-full.md`.

## Components

- Accordion — Sections behind headers that open and close: one at a time (`single`) or any number (`multiple`).
- AccordionItem — One section of an `Accordion`: its header and the content it opens.
- Alert — A message that stays put in the page - for what a toast would carry away too soon.
- AngleGlyph — The chevron that expands and collapses a branch.
- Badge — Compact status label; as a `pill` for counters.
- Breadcrumb — Where a page stands: the trail from the root to the current page.
- Button — A button with a label.
- ButtonGroup — Buttons standing side by side as one control: square inside, rounded outside, a hairline between them.
- CalendarGlyph — A calendar leaf: a sheet with its header and two rings.
- Card — A surface that groups content under a head: `CardHeader` and `CardBody` inside a `section`.
- CardBody — The content of a `Card`; the part that folds away in a collapsible card.
- CardHeader — The head of a `Card`: title, an optional eyebrow and actions on the right.
- Checkbox — A native checkbox with its label beside it; `indeterminate` shows the mixed state.
- ClockGlyph — A clock face at a quarter past - the time pickers' character.
- Combobox — A searchable select field (the combobox pattern with a listbox panel).
- CommandPalette — A window above the application in which one types and finds: it searches `items` by subsequence, ranks and marks the matches, and reports the chosen candidate's `id` to `onChoose`.
- ConfirmDialog — Compact confirmation dialog on the basis of Modal.
- ContextMenu — A menu that opens at a point instead of under a trigger – for a right-click on something that is not a button: a canvas, a row, a subtask.
- ControlSizeProvider — Sets the size of the controls inside it - every control with `size` takes it unless it says its own.
- CrossGlyph — The cross that clears a field, removes a tag or a chip, and closes a dialog, a toast or an alert - one drawing at the size of its place.
- DatePicker — A date selection with a calendar panel; weeks begin on Monday.
- DateRangePicker — The time span selection - the convenience component of the library.
- DateTimePicker — A date and a time of day in one field, with a calendar panel.
- DateTimeRangePicker — A time span with times at both ends - the same interaction logic as the DateRangePicker, extended by times, without sacrificing the lightness: - Two clicks set the days (backwards allowed, the ends swap silently), after which the focus jumps straight into the first time field: type 0800, slide on, type 1700, Enter - done.
- Divider — A line between rows or columns; with `label` a named separator.
- Drawer — A `Modal` that stands at an edge of the window and enters from it - the same dialog with the same head, body and foot.
- EmptyState — An empty surface as an invitation to act - not as a dead end.
- FileInput — A file field with a drop zone: choose or drop files, and remove them from the list again.
- FormField — A label, a hint or error and the control they belong to.
- FormFieldBoundary — Resets the FormField context.
- Grid — Children in a grid: a fixed number of columns, a list of column widths, or as many columns as fit at `minItemWidth`.
- GridGlyph — The grid laid over an area.
- GripGlyph — The grip a drag is taken by: two grooves.
- Heading — A heading whose level and size are independent: a small heading can still be the second level of the page.
- IconButton — A button that shows only an icon: square, named by its `aria-label`, and that name shown as a tooltip.
- Input — A one-line text field.
- LanguageProvider — Sets formatting and wording for the subtree below.
- Link — A link in the size of its text; `external` opens it in a new tab with the protective attributes set.
- MeasureGlyph — Measuring: a line with two end marks.
- Menu — A dropdown menu: `trigger` opens a panel of `MenuItem`s below it.
- MenuItem — An entry of a `Menu` or a `ContextMenu`.
- MenuSeparator — Fine dividing line between groups of menu entries.
- Meter — A narrow fill-level bar, e.g. for utilisation in table cells.
- MinusGlyph — The minus of `NumberInput`'s step buttons.
- Modal — A dialog window in the middle of the screen, on the native `<dialog>`: focus trap, Escape and scroll lock come from the browser.
- ModalBody — The content of a `Modal` or `Drawer` - the part that scrolls while head and foot stand fast.
- ModalFooter — The foot of a `Modal` or `Drawer`, for its buttons; they stand at the end.
- ModalHeader — The head of a `Modal` or `Drawer`: its title, which names the dialog for a screen reader, an optional sentence below it, and the close cross.
- MultiSelect — Multiple selection.
- NumberInput — Number input in the notation of the formats (`useFormats`): its decimal and group separators are read and written, the group separators set on leaving and tolerated while typing.
- PlusGlyph — The plus of `NumberInput`'s step buttons.
- Popover — The one closable, anchored surface.
- ProgressBar — How far a task has come, from 0 to 1 - or, without a value, that it runs.
- RadioGroup — One choice out of a few possibilities, all of them visible.
- SegmentedControl — One choice out of a few short possibilities, drawn as one field with a segment for each - a radio group in what it says and how the keys move.
- Select — The select field.
- Skeleton — Loading placeholder with a restrained pulse; respects prefers-reduced-motion.
- Slider — One value on a track between `min` and `max`, on the native range input; `marks` set labelled points on the track.
- Sparkline — A miniature trend line for table cells; the end point carries the accent.
- Spinner — Restrained loading indicator; inherits the text colour of its context.
- SplitButton — A main action with a menu of its variants: the button on the left runs `onClick`, the arrow on the right opens `menu`.
- Splitter — Two panes and the line between them.
- Stack — Children below or beside one another, spaced by a step of the spacing scale.
- Stat — Metric tile: one value, read against its limits.
- Stepper — Where a procedure stands: its steps in order, done, current, upcoming or failed.
- Switch — An on/off switch that takes effect at once - a native checkbox with `role="switch"`.
- Tab — One tab of a `TabList`; choosing it shows the `TabPanel` of the same `value`.
- TabList — The row of `Tab`s inside `Tabs`.
- TabPanel — The content of one tab.
- Tabs — Tabs: a `TabList` of `Tab`s and one `TabPanel` per value.
- Tag — A short label with a tone; with `onRemove` it carries a cross that removes it.
- TagGroup — A list of `Tag`s with one tab stop: the arrow keys move between them, and the focus stays in the group when one is removed.
- Text — Body text from the token set; `as` chooses the element, independent of the size.
- Textarea — A text field for several lines.
- ToastProvider — Holds the toasts and draws them; `useToast` works only below it.
- Tooltip — Appears on hover and keyboard focus; purely descriptive (role="tooltip").
- TreeSearch — The search field of a tree: an `Input` that writes into the tree's search text, so that the `TreeView` filters on it.
- TreeView — The tree as a control: draws the nodes of a `Tree` from `useTree` as a `tree` with keyboard navigation, typeahead and, with `checkable`, ticks.
- UmrissProvider — The root provider.
- VisuallyHidden — Content for the screen reader only: out of sight, but read out.

## Hooks

- useCommandPaletteShortcut — Binds Cmd+K/Ctrl+K and "/" to the opening of a palette.
- useDensity — The configured density; without a provider, "comfortable".
- useDensityFor — A component's `density`: its own value, else the density the provider explicitly configured, else its own default.
- useFormField — Used by Input, Select and Checkbox to wire up id and aria automatically.
- useFormats — Only the formatting.
- useFreshness — Judges a value's as-of time continuously and causes a redraw when it changes state.
- useLanguage — Formatting and wording together; without a provider, the defaults.
- usePortalTarget — Resolves the configured portal target.
- useToast — Access to `toast()`, `update()` and `dismiss()`; requires a ToastProvider.
- useToastConfig — The setting for toasts; without a provider, empty.
- useTree — The state of a tree - which branches are open, which nodes are checked, the active node, the anchor and the search text - and the gestures on it.
- useUmriss — The whole configuration; without a provider, the defaults.
- useVirtual — Holds the scroll position and the measured heights and derives the window from them.
- useWording — Only the wording.

## Props

### Accordion

#### props (AccordionProps)

- type?: "single" | "multiple" — `single` keeps at most one section open, `multiple` any number. (default: "single")
- value?: readonly string[] — Controlled: the values of the open sections.
- defaultValue?: readonly string[] — Uncontrolled: the sections open at the start. (default: [])
- onChange?: (value: string[]) => void — Reports the open sections after a header's click.
- headingLevel?: 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 — The level of the heading each header stands in - the one that fits the page's outline. (default: 3)
- ref?: Ref<HTMLDivElement> — Receives the component's root element.

### ConfirmDialog

#### props (ConfirmDialogProps)

- open: boolean — Whether the query stands.
