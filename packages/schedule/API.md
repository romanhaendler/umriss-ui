# @umriss-ui/schedule API

Generated by the props step (`pnpm --filter @umriss-ui/schedule props`); do not edit. Lists every export, and the props, options and returns of each component and hook, with first sentences only; the full text is `docs/llms-full.md`. What stands under `## Experimental`, or is marked `(experimental)`, may change in a minor release; everything else is the surface a break costs a major for (ADR-0083).

## Hooks

- useSchedule — Declares a schedule: hands back `Schedule` and the parts declared inside it - `Lane`, `LaneGroup`, `Subtasks`, `Dependencies`, `BlockedTimes`, `Milestones`, `DataTable` -, its `view` and the setters `setView`, `setDomain`, `toggleGroup`, `foldAll` and `unfoldAll`, `positionAt`, `clientPointOf` and `visibleDomain`, and `scrollToLane`, `scrollToSubtask` and `focusSubtask`.

## Functions

- applyIntent — The subtask as the intent asks for it.
- arrival — When a dependency has to have arrived at its subtask, by its anchor.
- departure — When a dependency leaves its subtask, by its anchor.
- findings — Every finding at once.
- inBlockedTime — Every subtask that covers blocked time on its lane, lead-in and lead-out included - once per blocked interval it covers, in the order of the subtasks.
- occupied — The interval a subtask occupies on its lane: lead-in and lead-out included.
- overlaps — Every pair of subtasks that cover each other on a lane.
- resolveAppearance — Settles a subtask's appearances into what is drawn.
- ripple — The moves that push every successor whose dependency no longer fits after `intent` - transitively, in the order they were first pushed.
- shiftTask — The moves that carry a whole task in time - every subtask of it by the same amount, so that its lengths and the distances between its stops stay as they were.
- snapTime — `time` onto the nearest line of the raster in the zone's time, the reader's unless one is named - a step alone starts at the zone's midnight.
- subtaskFromPlace — The subtask a place intent asks for, under an id the caller chooses.
- violatedDependencies — Every dependency whose successor is to be reached before the predecessor's departure plus the dependency's lag.

## Options and returns

### useSchedule

#### options (ScheduleOptions)

- initialView?: ScheduleView — The view to start from, read once when the hook first runs; a different value on a later render is ignored, as React ignores a changed `defaultValue`.
- onViewChange?: (view: ScheduleView) => void — Every change the planner makes, once, always the whole view - the one to keep, or to hand another schedule's `setView`.

#### returns (ScheduleParts)

- Schedule: (props: ScheduleProps) => ReactNode — The schedule.
- Lane: typeof Lane
- LaneGroup: typeof LaneGroup
- Subtasks: typeof Subtasks
- Dependencies: typeof Dependencies
- BlockedTimes: typeof BlockedTimes
- Milestones: typeof Milestones
- DataTable: typeof DataTable — The plan as a table, on demand: placed inside the `Schedule`, it gives the schedule a "Show data" key beside the bottom time band, and the key opens a table of the subtasks in view - lane, task, subtask, from and to with their dates, duration and findings in words.
- view: ScheduleView — How the planner is looking at the plan: the span and the folded groups.
- setView: (view: ScheduleView) => void — Goes to a whole view; what it leaves out is shown whole again.
- setDomain: (span: readonly [number, number] | null) => void — Puts a span of two wall-clock instants in view; `null` shows the subtasks' extent.
- toggleGroup: (id: string) => void — Folds or unfolds one **Lane group**.
- positionAt: (clientX: number, clientY: number) => { time: number; lane: string | null } | n… — The time and the lane at a client point, or null outside the plot - and null while no schedule is mounted.
- clientPointOf: (time: number, lane?: string) => { x: number; y: number } | null — The client point of a time - on the middle of a lane where one is named - or null while the schedule is not on screen.
- visibleDomain: () => readonly [number, number] — The visible span, as two wall-clock instants.
- scrollToLane: (lane: string) => boolean — Scrolls the lanes until the lane's row is in view, and returns `true`; `false` and nothing changed for an unknown lane, one inside a folded **Lane group** (none is unfolded), or while no schedule is mounted.
- scrollToSubtask: (id: string) => boolean — Brings the subtask's lane and time into view, the span moving to it and keeping its width as `setDomain` would, and leaves the focus where it is; `false` and nothing changed in the cases of `scrollToLane`.
- focusSubtask: (id: string) => boolean — Does what `scrollToSubtask` does, then makes the subtask the **Active subtask** and moves the focus to the plot, where the live region reads it out as the arrow keys do; `false` and nothing changed in the cases of `scrollToLane`.
- foldAll: () => void — Folds every group.
- unfoldAll: () => void — Unfolds every group.

#### props of Schedule (ScheduleProps)

- ref?: Ref<HTMLDivElement> | undefined — Receives the root element.
- height?: number — Height in CSS pixels, both bands included, fixed. (default: fills the frame, at least 400 px)
- laneHeight?: number — Height of one lane in pixels at the default font size of 16 px; the schedule multiplies it by the root font size over 16 px when that ratio is above 1, so lane names keep their room in a reader's larger font. (default: 44)
- headerWidth?: number — Width of the lane headers in pixels - at most 28 % of the schedule (never under 88 pixels), so that on a phone the plot keeps the larger part. (default: 160)
- calendar?: CalendarInput — A working calendar: the intervals in which time counts. (default: [])
- zoomLimits?: ZoomLimits — The narrowest and widest time span zoom may reach, in milliseconds. (default: one hour to 28 days)
- snap?: "ticks" | number | SnapRaster | false — The raster a drag lands on: `"ticks"` - the fine band's current step -, a step in milliseconds from local midnight, a step with an offset (shifts at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00 are eight hours offset by six), or `false` for none. (default: "ticks")
- intents?: readonly IntentKind[] — The intents the caller handles. (default: [])
- canMoveTo?: (subtask: Subtask, lane: string) => boolean — Whether a subtask may go to a lane. (default: every lane is open)
- onIntent?: (intent: Intent) => void — Called once per intent when a drag ends.
- onInteraction?: (interaction: ScheduleInteraction) => void — Click, context menu and hover, with what the pointer is on and where.
- selectedTask?: string | null — The selected task, controlled.
- onSelectedTaskChange?: (task: string | null, subtask: string | null) => void — Called when a click selects a task or clears the selection, with the subtask that was clicked - null where the click was on a dependency or on nothing.
- placing?: PlacingItem | null — What the application is dragging in from outside while it drags it - its key, task, the length of its main time, its lead-in and lead-out.
- route?: DependencyRoute — How the dependencies are drawn: a `"curve"` that leaves and arrives forwards, a `"straight"` line, or `"orthogonal"` segments. (default: "curve")
- attach?: DependencyAttachment — Where a dependency's ends sit on their bars: the `"centre"` of both, or the `"nearest"` edge - which is the shortest line between two stops. (default: "centre")
- endMarks?: DependencyEnds — Whether a dependency's two ends carry a dot (`"dot"`) or the line stands alone (`"none"`). (default: "dot")
- barLabel?: (subtask: Subtask) => string — What stands written in a bar: a function from a subtask to a line of text, or nothing for bars without text.
- tooltip?: false | ((target: ScheduleTooltipTarget) => ReactNode) — The tooltip on a hovered subtask, milestone or dependency: its order, its times, its parts and its findings.
- now?: boolean | number — A line marking the present across the lanes: `true` follows the clock by the minute, an instant fixes it there. (default: false)
- loading?: boolean — The plan is on its way, as the chart's `loading` says ([ADR-0042](https://github.com/romanhaendler/umriss-ui/blob/main/docs/adr/0042-a-rows-height-never-follows-what-it-shows.md)). (default: false)
- children?: ReactNode — `Lane`, `LaneGroup`, `Subtasks`, `Dependencies`, `BlockedTimes`, `Milestones` and `DataTable`, in the order they are to stand and be drawn.
- ends?: DependencyEnds — The old name of `endMarks`. (deprecated)
- label?: (subtask: Subtask) => string — The old name of `barLabel`. (deprecated)

#### props of Lane (LaneProps)

- id: string — The identity subtasks name in their `lane`.
- label?: ReactNode — What the lane header says - the person, vehicle or room. (default: id)

#### props of LaneGroup (LaneGroupProps)

- id: string — The group's identity - what the view's `folded` names.
- label?: ReactNode — What the group's header says - the team, the depot, the region. (default: id)
- children?: ReactNode — `Lane`s and further `LaneGroup`s, to any depth.

#### props of Subtasks (SubtasksProps)

- data: readonly Subtask[] — The subtasks, as the caller holds them.
- tasks: readonly Task[] — The tasks the subtasks belong to - their identity and their colour.

#### props of Dependencies (DependenciesProps)

- data: readonly Dependency[] — The dependencies, as the caller holds them.

#### props of BlockedTimes (BlockedTimesProps)

- data: readonly BlockedTime[] — The blocked time of the lanes - leave, maintenance, unavailability -, as the caller holds it: one list, each interval naming its lane.

#### props of Milestones (MilestonesProps)

- data: readonly Milestone[] — The milestones of the lanes, as the caller holds them: one list, each naming its lane and the instant it stands at.
